Common questions

  • Was a credit used when a review failed? No. A credit is only used when a review finishes. If it fails, times out or you tap Stop, it's returned automatically.
  • I bought credits but they didn't appear. Open the app again while online; purchases are confirmed on launch. If they still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase.
  • How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Is my resume stored? No. It stays on your iPhone, your personal details are masked before it's reviewed, and we don't keep it afterwards. See the Privacy Policy.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Account → Delete account.
