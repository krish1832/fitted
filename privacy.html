Fitted

Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Fitted helps you tailor your resume to a job. We built it to use as little of your information as possible. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use the app.

In short

  • Your resume file and saved results stay on your device.
  • To write your suggestions, your resume text and the job description are sent to an AI service run by Groq, or by Google if Groq is unavailable. This happens only after you give permission.
  • Your name, email, phone number, links, addresses, ID numbers and date of birth are removed on your device before anything is sent.
  • We don't keep your resume or the job description after your review.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your information.

What stays on your device

Your resume file, the job descriptions you paste and your saved results are kept on your device only. You can clear your history at any time, and deleting the app removes it.

How your suggestions are made

When you tap Tailor my resume, the app reads the text of your resume on your device and removes your personal details: your name, email address, phone number, links, addresses, ID numbers and date of birth. Each one is replaced with a placeholder such as [EMAIL].

What is sent: the rest of your resume text, such as your work history, employers, job titles, education and skills, and the job description you pasted.

Who it is sent to: it goes over a secure connection to our server, which is hosted by Supabase, and from there to one of these AI providers:

  • Groq, Inc. (groq.com), which runs the AI model that writes your suggestions;
  • Google LLC, through the Gemini API, used only when Groq is unavailable.

How it is used: only to write your suggestions, your estimated match score and the list of skills the job asks for. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and we don't use it to build a profile of you. Your personal details are put back into the suggestions on your device, so they read naturally. The AI providers never see them.

Your permission: the app asks for your permission, and names these providers, before it sends anything for the first time. You can withdraw it at any time in Account → Allow AI review; the app will then ask again before it sends anything.

What the AI providers may do with it: Groq and Google handle what they receive under their own terms and privacy policies (groq.com/privacy-policy and policies.google.com/privacy). Google may use what it receives through the Gemini API to improve its products, and people at Google may read it. This is one reason your personal details are removed before anything is sent. If you don't want this, don't allow AI review.

We don't keep what you send. Your finished suggestions are held for a short time, up to two days, so that an interrupted review can be shown again without using another credit, and are then deleted.

What we keep about your account

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Apple, we receive a private account ID from Apple, and your email address only if you choose to share it.
  • Your credits. Your credit balance and a record of credits added and used.
  • Your purchases. A record of what you bought and when. Apple handles payment, so we never see your card or bank details.
  • Basic service records. For example, when a review happened and whether it worked, so we can keep the app running well. These don't include your resume and are deleted after 90 days.

To give free credits only once per iPhone, we ask Apple to remember a simple yes or no for your device. We don't receive any information that identifies your device.

Who helps us run the app

We use a small number of trusted service providers:

  • Supabase, which hosts our server and stores your account and credits;
  • Groq, Inc., which receives your resume text without your personal details, and the job description, to write your suggestions;
  • Google LLC (Gemini API), which receives the same text only when Groq is unavailable;
  • Apple, for signing in and payments.

We only share what each of them needs to do its job. Each of them is bound by its own terms and privacy policy to keep what it receives secure and to use it only to provide its service to us, except as described above for Google.

How long we keep information

  • Your resume and job descriptions: we don't keep them.
  • Your suggestions: up to two days.
  • Service records: 90 days.
  • Your account, credits and purchases: until you delete your account.

Deleting your account

Go to Account → Delete account in the app. This deletes your account and credits and erases what the app saved on your iPhone. Any remaining credits are lost. We keep purchase records without your name or account attached, because the law requires records of sales. For refunds, contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your choices and rights

You can see and delete your information in the app at any time. Depending on where you live, for example in India or the European Union, you may also have the right to ask what information we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email us at krish.bhingradiya@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Fitted is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Keeping your information safe

Your information is sent over secure connections, and we keep as little as we can. No service can be completely secure, but collecting less means there is less to protect.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top and let you know in the app about any important changes.

Contact
